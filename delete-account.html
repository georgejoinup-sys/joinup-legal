Delete your account

JoinUp, by Testcamp Inc.

From inside the app

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Deleting your account cancels your Joinups, removes you from rosters, anonymizes your profile, and removes your invites, memberships, and device tokens. Your phone number is cleared from your account record. No email or phone call is needed for this in-app path — it is fully self-service.

Without the app

If you have uninstalled JoinUp, or no longer have access to it, you can still request deletion of your account and its data by email.

Email: support@joinup.com

This mailbox is monitored by a person, and we answer every message.

Include the phone number on your account (or the last 4 digits) so we can find it.

Never send a one-time sign-in code or a password — we do not need either to delete your account.

What deletion removes

Whichever route you use, what deletion removes — and what stays — is described in section 7 of the Privacy Policy, “Retention and account deletion”.
