How it works

From “up for it?” to locked in.

No group-chat wrangling, no maybes, no plans that die in the scroll. Four steps, then you’re there.

  1. 01

    Say what you’re up for

    Coffee, a run, pickup football, a study session. Post it in seconds — what, where, when, and how many people you want.

  2. 02

    The people you choose join

    Your Joinup shows up for the people you choose. Whoever’s in taps join — you see the count fill up as it happens.

  3. 03

    It locks in

    When enough people are in, the plan locks. Everyone’s confirmed, everyone knows it’s on — no flaking into the void.

  4. 04

    You meet

    Show up and hang out. That’s the whole point — JoinUp ends where real life begins.

Trust & safety

Real people. Real accountability.

Meeting people you don’t know yet only works when the people are real — and the tools are there the moment something’s wrong.

Phone-verified accounts

Every account is tied to a real phone number, verified by SMS code. Sign-in is that code — there’s no password for you to remember or reuse, and no anonymous drive-by accounts.

Face Check — before your first Joinup

The first time someone hosts or joins a Joinup, JoinUp will ask for a short video selfie confirming a live person is really there — not a photo or a recording. Signing up needs only a phone number. It is designed so JoinUp never sees the video: it goes from the phone to a verification provider, and JoinUp keeps only the result of the check.

Block and report, built in

Report or block anyone from their profile. Reports are confidential, and a person reviews every one — removing content and ejecting offenders when a report is substantiated.
