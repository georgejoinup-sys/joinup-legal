1. Who may claim a business

  • You must be at least 18 years old and have a JoinUp account that we have not suspended or banned.
  • You must be the owner of the business, or a person the owner has authorized to claim and manage its page, and you must have the authority to bind the business to these Business Terms. When you accept for a business, "you" means both you and that business.
  • The business must be a real business that operates lawfully at, or serves customers from, the location shown on its page.
  • A business has one business account. A business with several locations manages all of them from that one account. Do not open a second account for the same business, and do not claim a location that is not part of your business.
  • You may not claim a page if we have previously removed your access to JoinUp for Business.

2. People who manage your page

  • The owner of the business account may invite employees and others to help manage the business's pages. Each person uses their own JoinUp account; do not share accounts or sign-in codes, and sign out of JoinUp on any device other people can use.
  • Everyone you invite acts on behalf of your business, under the owner's account. Your business is responsible for everything they do in JoinUp for Business as if the business had done it, and for making sure they follow these Business Terms.
  • Remove a person's access when they leave your business or should no longer act for it, and tell us promptly if you believe someone has used your business account without permission.

3. Verification

We verify a claim by checking control of a contact channel that is already publicly listed for the business, not by accepting what a claimant tells us. The usual method is a short-lived, single-use code delivered to the phone number already listed for that business. A phone number that a claimant types in does not verify a claim.

When the listed number cannot be used, or when a claim is disputed, we may ask for other evidence, such as a code sent by mail to the listed address, or documents showing that you own or run the business. Not every method is available for every business or at every time. We do not promise that a claim will be verified, or verified by any particular date.

Do not interfere with verification. Do not request or intercept a code meant for someone else, change a business's publicly listed phone number or address in order to pass verification, or submit false or altered documents.

We keep a record of each claim, including who made it, when, by which method, and on what evidence, so that the claim can be reviewed later.

4. Refusing, suspending or removing a claim

We may refuse a claim, suspend access to a claimed page, or remove a claim if: we cannot verify it; we reasonably believe the claimant lacks authority or gave us false information; the business has closed, moved or changed hands; the claim is disputed; your business or anyone acting for it breaks these Business Terms, our Terms of Service or the law; or the law requires it. Where it is practical and lawful, we will tell you the reason.

When a claim is removed, the page can stay on JoinUp as an unclaimed listing, and Business Content already on it may stay, be changed or be removed as described in sections 6 and 7.

5. Disputes between claimants

Only one business account may hold the claim to a page at a time. If more than one person or business says it has the right to manage a page, we may pause the page, keep the existing claim in place while we look into it, ask each side for evidence, or move the claim. We decide on the evidence we have, and we do not promise any particular outcome or timing.

We are not responsible for settling disagreements between owners, partners, former employees, franchisors, landlords or buyers of a business; those are for the people involved to resolve.
